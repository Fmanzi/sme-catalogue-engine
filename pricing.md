# Pricing & Plans

The platform helps small businesses run an online catalogue with **zero upfront cost**.
We host on free infrastructure (Cloudflare Pages + Worker + GitHub), so our own costs
stay near zero — and we pass that saving on to you.

All plans are **pay-monthly (or pay-per-year at ~2 months free)**, billed in **KES**
(Kenyan Shilling). No setup fees. You can start Free and upgrade any time.

## What every plan includes (even Free)

These are the basics — Free is a real store, not just a trial:

- **Live, mobile-friendly storefront** you can customise.
- **Admin dashboard** to add / edit / remove products, categories, collections, brands,
  statuses, pricing and images.
- **Photo upload** — drag & drop, with automatic compression (WebP/AVIF) so your pages
  load fast.
- **Homepage (hero) editing** — change the banner images and text.
- **Theme colours** — pick your brand colours and logo.
- **Order intake + notifications** straight to your Telegram/WhatsApp.
- **Automatic backups** — every change is versioned (your store can be restored).
- **Free hosting** — you pay nothing for bandwidth or storage on our free tier.

## Package details

### 🟢 Free — KSh 0/month
For trying the platform or very small shops.

- Live store with the platform's "Powered by" credit.
- Up to **30 products**, in up to **3 categories/collections**.
- 1 staff login (the owner).
- Standard theme, standard domain (`yourstore.pages.dev`).
- Order notifications included.
- Email support.

**Good first step:** you get a complete, working shop for nothing. Upgrade when you
need more products, a domain, or to remove the credit.

### 🔵 Pro — KSh 1,200/month
For small shops ready to look professional and grow.

- Everything in Free, **plus**:
- Up to **500 products**, unlimited categories/collections/brands.
- **2 staff logins**.
- **Your own domain** connected.
- **Remove the "Powered by" credit**.
- **CSV bulk import** to add hundreds of products at once.
- Priority email support.

### 🟣 Business — KSh 3,500/month
For busy or multi-brand shops that need live stock and to sell from a physical shop too.

- Everything in Pro, **plus**:
- **Unlimited products** and up to **10 staff logins**.
- **Live stock tracking** — inventory updates the moment a sale is made.
- **POS sync** — sales at a physical counter and online share the same stock & orders.
- **API access** for integrations, extra terminals, or external tools.
- **Priority support** (WhatsApp/Telegram, faster response).

**The Business plan is the one that powers our own "online + physical shop" setup.**

## Choosing a plan — quick guide

- Starting out, testing the platform, or fewer than ~30 products → **Free**.
- Building a real brand, want your own domain and no credit → **Pro**.
- Selling a lot, tracking stock live, or running a physical shop alongside online → **Business**.

## Our zero-cost commitment

The platform runs entirely on free infrastructure:

- **Cloudflare Pages** hosts the storefront and admin — no server to pay for.
- **Cloudflare Worker** is the API "gate" — no database server.
- **GitHub** is the storage/backups — every save is a version.

This means our costs don't grow much as stores grow, so we can keep the **Free tier
genuinely free** and the paid tiers affordable for small businesses in Kenya.

## Upgrade / downgrade

- Upgrade any time — new features unlock immediately (prorated for the month).
- Downgrade — changes apply at the next billing cycle; you won't lose data, you may
  just go over the new limits (e.g. keep reading, can't add new products past the cap).
- Cancel any time — you keep your domain and can export your data.
